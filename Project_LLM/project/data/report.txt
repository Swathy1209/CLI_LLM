BUSINESS PERFORMANCE REPORT — Q4 2024
Prepared by: Business Intelligence Team
Date: December 2024

=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===

The company experienced mixed performance in Q4 2024. While the North region maintained
steady growth and Engineering continued to scale headcount for upcoming product launches,
the South region faced significant headwinds driven by competitive pressure and supply chain
disruptions. Overall net revenue declined 12% month-over-month in November compared to
August, requiring immediate attention from regional leadership.

=== PARAGRAPH 1: REVENUE OVERVIEW ===

Total net revenue across all regions and products peaked in August 2024 at approximately
$136,750 and has trended downward through November, reaching roughly $112,160. This
represents a cumulative decline of approximately 18% over the four-month period. The North
region bucked this trend, posting consistent growth in Widget C sales, which reached $26,400
in net revenue in November — the single strongest product-region combination on record.

=== PARAGRAPH 2: SOUTH REGION DECLINE ===

The South region has been the primary driver of revenue deterioration. South region net revenue
fell from $62,800 in August to $37,150 in November — a drop of more than 40%. The root causes
identified by the regional sales team include: (1) increased competition from a local distributor
offering Widget A substitutes at 20% lower price points; (2) a logistics disruption in October
that delayed Widget C shipments, causing customers to seek alternatives; and (3) a spike in
product returns, particularly for Widget C, suggesting possible quality control issues in that
region's distribution chain. Sales leadership has escalated the returns issue to the Operations
team for investigation.

=== PARAGRAPH 3: SALARY AND HEADCOUNT TRENDS ===

Total payroll expenses have risen steadily throughout the period, growing from $585,850 in
August to $637,340 in November — an increase of 8.8%. Engineering headcount grew from 40
to 45 employees, reflecting planned hiring for the company's platform migration project
scheduled for Q1 2025. The Sales department added 2 headcount in October to support
expansion into new territories. While these investments are strategic, the combination of
rising payroll and falling revenue has compressed operating margins significantly. The
Finance team has flagged this as a concern if South region revenue does not recover in Q1.

=== PARAGRAPH 4: OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS ===

The Operations team completed a warehouse consolidation in the West region during October,
which is expected to reduce fulfillment costs by approximately 5% starting in Q1 2025. The
HR department is finalizing a revised bonus structure for the Sales team, aimed at incentivizing
performance in underperforming regions. Marketing spent Q4 piloting two digital campaigns
targeting enterprise customers, with results expected in January 2025. Early indicators suggest
a 15% improvement in lead quality compared to prior campaigns.

=== PARAGRAPH 5: OUTLOOK ===

Management remains cautiously optimistic for Q1 2025. The North region is expected to
continue outperforming, and the Widget B product line shows resilience in Eastern markets.
Recovery in the South region will depend on resolution of the supply chain and returns issues.
The company's cost structure must be carefully managed given the rising payroll baseline. If
revenue does not recover to August levels by Q2 2025, the executive team will consider
restructuring options including a review of South region headcount and possible promotional
pricing on Widget A to recapture lost market share.
